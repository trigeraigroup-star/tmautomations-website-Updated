# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated to the Sites scaffold selected by the user. The first review build remains local and must not be published without explicit approval.

## Users

Owners and operators of small and mid-size businesses across industries. They are not buying AI technology for its own sake; they want relief from repetitive checking, chasing, copying, and administrative work.

## Product Purpose

TMAutomations builds practical AI agents and workflow automations across sales, operations, finance, and administration. It also offers AI-assisted marketing, lead generation, and website SEO/AEO.

## Positioning

TMAutomations acts as the client's automation team: an honest, full-service partner that finds operational friction, builds around the way the business actually works, trains the team, and stays involved as the business changes.

## Operating Context

Typical client work includes email follow-up, quotes, invoices, supplier coordination, lead routing, client updates, internal status checks, company procedures, and business documents. Prospective clients may run restaurants, manufacturing businesses, showrooms, notary practices, or other service businesses.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page, mobile-first marketing site with a contact destination and future blog support.
- Five separate short video scenes will eventually support a scroll-led narrative; the draft uses clearly labeled placeholders.
- All meaningful labels and story copy must remain semantic HTML rather than baked into video.
- Videos will be muted, lazy-loaded WebM/MP4 with poster images; mobile will favor posters.
- No pricing, fabricated metrics, invented ROI, fake testimonials, fake dashboards, or unsupported claims.
- Primary CTA is “Let’s connect,” targeting the on-page contact form (approved 2026-09-07).
- Hero headline is “There’s another way this day could go.” (approved 2026-09-07).
- Never use “AIOS” or “AI operating system.”

## Brand Commitments

- Company name: TMAutomations, carrying the meaning “your automation team.”
- Founder: Michal Triger, a Los Angeles-based solo consultant with about 15 years of operations experience and fluency in English and Hebrew.
- Voice: plain, honest, professional, practical, and non-technical.
- The company is the brand; do not show Michal's face or use personal photography.
- Visual world: warm cream, deep ink, sage, bright olive, and sparing light blue; the How We Work chapter shifts to midnight slate, amber, and linen-like physical texture.
- Physical craft imagery only: paper, pencil, whiteboards, cards, and an amber desk lamp. No robots, holograms, glowing orbs, neon, or generic AI imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

Founder experience only for the first draft. Client logos, testimonials, results, and project examples will be supplied later and must remain explicit placeholders until then.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the work in language a restaurant owner would understand.
2. Show how work becomes orderly instead of making inflated outcome promises.
3. Make the client feel guided rather than sold a mysterious technology.
4. Keep proof honest and visibly incomplete until real material is supplied.
5. Treat speed, accessibility, and search readability as part of the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark palettes, semantic heading order, keyboard-operable controls, meaningful alt text, and a complete reduced-motion experience are required.
